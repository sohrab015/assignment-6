Frequently Asked Questions

1. How can I start learning English on this website?
You can start by exploring our vocabulary lessons. Learn new English words with their Bengali meanings, pronunciation, examples, and interactive exercises.
2. Is this website suitable for beginners?
Yes! English Janala is designed for beginners. You can learn English step by step with easy explanations, Bengali translations, and practical examples.
3. Do I need to create an account?
No, you don't need an account to explore our available learning resources. You can start learning English directly from our website.
4. How can I improve my English vocabulary?
Practice regularly by learning new words, understanding their meanings, reading example sentences, and completing interactive vocabulary exercises.
5. Do you provide certificates for completing courses?
Currently, English Janala focuses on helping learners improve their English vocabulary through interactive lessons and practice activities. Certificate availability may be introduced in the future.
